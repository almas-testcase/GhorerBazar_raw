    Campaigns

    Send an offer to your past customers by SMS or email — in 3 easy steps.

    What is a campaign?

    A campaign is a message you send to a group of customers to bring them back to your shop — for example a festival offer, a "we miss you" note, or news about new products. You can add a coupon and show the same offer in your website's top banner.

    1. 1. Choose whoAll customers, repeat buyers, people who stopped ordering…
    2. 2. Write the messageUse a ready template, add a coupon, preview it.
    3. 3. Review & sendSend a test to yourself first, then send to everyone.

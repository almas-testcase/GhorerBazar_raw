The security of your information is important to us.

What Information We Collect

To process orders, we collect your name, mobile number, address and order details.

How We Use Your Information

Only for order confirmation, delivery and customer service. Your information is never sold or shared with third parties (except delivery partners, as far as necessary).

Cookies & Ad Tracking (Meta Pixel)

We may use the Meta (Facebook) Pixel to measure the effectiveness of our ads. It may send information such as which products you viewed, added to cart or purchased (without your name, phone or address) to Meta. If "Do Not Track" is enabled in your browser, we do not run the Pixel.

Reviews

Customers who have received their delivery can leave a review. Only the first part of your name is shown; your phone number or address is never published.

Order Notifications

To share order confirmation and status updates, we may send an SMS to your mobile number and, if provided, an email.

Security

Information is stored on secure servers and can only be viewed by authorised people.

Contact

If you want your information deleted, let us know by phone/WhatsApp.
